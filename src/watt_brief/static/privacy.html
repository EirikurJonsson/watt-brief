Privacy

This is a demo, so we keep as little as possible. Here is what happens to what you type and to your connection.

Your question

When you ask for a brief, your question is sent to a language model provider, DeepInfra, so the model can write the answer. DeepInfra handles it under its own terms. Do not type personal or confidential information.

We do not store your question. Our logs record how long it was and whether it worked, never its text.

Your connection

Your IP address is held in the server's memory to limit how many requests one visitor can make. It is not written to our logs or to any database, and it is gone when the server restarts. The site is served through Cloudflare, which handles traffic under its own terms.

What we do not use

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising.
